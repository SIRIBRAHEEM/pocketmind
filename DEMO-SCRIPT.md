# PocketMind demo script (about 2 minutes, max 3)

Record the screen at 1920x1080 in Chrome on desktop, with the Firebase
project connected and Gemini Nano available, so the On-device badges are
real. Speak slowly and clearly. Subtitles must match the narration word for
word. No face circle, no photo anywhere. Say the university in full:
"Federal University of Technology Minna". Never say the short form as one
word.

## 0:00 - Introduction (20 seconds)

"Hello, I am Ibrahim Yahaya from Bida, Niger State, Nigeria, a student at
the Federal University of Technology Minna. This is PocketMind, my personal
AI notebook. It answers questions from my own lecture notes, and it shows
me exactly where each answer was computed: on my device, or in the cloud."

Show: the PocketMind header and the Hybrid status panel. Point at the
on-device status badge.

## 0:20 - The problem (15 seconds)

"My notes are long, my data is expensive, and revision apps want me online.
PocketMind keeps my notes on this device, and runs the AI on the device
whenever Chrome can."

Show: the notes library with the three sample notes.

## 0:35 - Ask, on-device (30 seconds)

"I open my Photosynthesis note and ask: what traps light? Watch the badge.
On-device. Gemini Nano answered right here in Chrome. No network, no cost,
and my note never left this laptop."

Show: type the question, click Ask, point at the On-device badge on the
answer.

## 1:05 - Summary and flashcards (30 seconds)

"One tap gives me a revision summary. Another tap turns the note into
flashcards I can flip through. Same hybrid engine, same honest badge on
every result."

Show: Summarize note, then Make flashcards, flip one card.

## 1:35 - Cloud fallback (25 seconds)

"Now I switch the routing to cloud only, and ask again. This time the badge
says Cloud. Firebase AI Logic sent it to Gemini in the cloud. If I go
offline with cloud only on, PocketMind tells me plainly instead of failing
quietly. Long notes also go cloud first, because the on-device window is
small, and the app explains why."

Show: change Routing mode to Cloud only, ask a question, point at the Cloud
badge. Show the Hybrid status panel text.

## 2:00 - Close (20 seconds)

"PocketMind: your notes, answered on-device when possible, in the cloud
when needed, and honest about which one ran. Built with Firebase AI Logic
hybrid inference. Thank you."

Show: the header, the notes library, and one answer with its badge.
